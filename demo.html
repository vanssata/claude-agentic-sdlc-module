      What to watch
      
    The parts a model does not decide.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    The weight
    Seven lines before any edit. In the payment task, src/Payment/** is in the project's money modules, so domain is 2 and the task is full even though W is only 6.
    
      
    The scope refusal
    The typo task tries a second template. The hook refuses it and names the step. The agent either drops the edit or widens the task on record with a reason.
    
      
    The budget
    The payment suite goes red once. The failure is fixed as one batch, and the second run is the last one the agent gets. A third run would be your call.
    
      
    The dialog
    The question, the plan, the approval and the commit come to you in the runtime's own dialog. What you pick is recorded by a hook and runs once, verbatim.
